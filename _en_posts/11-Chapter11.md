THE LABORATORY OF MODERN PSYCHOTECHNOLOGIES at the Institute of Practical Psychology and Psychotherapy presents a specialized program “Psychotechnologies in Business”

The application program “Psychotechnologies in Business” sets itself the task of developing new approaches to communication, training and advisory support for business. First of all, it is addressed to specialists whose daily successful work largely depends on the ability to listen, understand and influence people.

## Structure of training seminars:

Sales technology

To whom it is addressed: Commercial agents (trade, advertising, real estate), sellers of company stores, heads of trade departments and companies. Objectives of the seminar Sales strategy: how to attract customers, talk about the product, convince them to make a purchase. Overcoming objections, speech techniques and strategies, using non-verbal behavior of the buyer. Effective Communication Strategy

Who is it addressed to: Professional communicators: managers at various levels, doctors, teachers, journalists. Objectives of the seminar Features of professional communication: patterns of attention, perception, speech. A step-by-step scheme leading to co-direction and satisfaction of interests, the formation of common goals. Modeling behavior, creating an atmosphere of trust and agreement. Persuasion technology

**To whom it is addressed:** politicians, top and middle managers, lawyers, journalists.

**Objectives of the seminar:** mastering the skills of professional communication and influencing the interlocutor: collecting information about the partner, entering into confidential contact, the ability to persuade and convince, negotiating and making decisions, changing the behavior and emotional reactions of the interlocutor.

Collaboration technology For company employees.

Business consulting. Organizing a team of like-minded people: organizational structure, assessing employee reliability, struggle for leadership, intra-company conflicts, effective work with clients. Optimizing interaction at various levels.

Inquiry technology

Lawyers, customs officers, security department employees of companies. Objectives of the seminar Expansion of sensory experience, calibration of “yes/no” states, technique of constructing questions, acceleration of required reactions, training of psychological stability, development of the ability to mobilize one’s resources in extreme situations.

Training in the program is carried out in the form of specialized training seminars according to the scheme 1/2 theory + 1/2 practice. The duration of the seminars is 20-38 hours (depending on the goals, objectives and form of delivery). The number of participants is no more than 15 people. On-site classes are available.

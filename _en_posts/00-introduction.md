# Introduction

## What are election campaign technologies?

Election campaign technologies are the body of methods, techniques, and tools used to organize and run election campaigns. They include strategic planning, electorate analysis, communication strategy, agitation and outreach, and much more.

## Why does this matter?

In today’s world, the success of an election campaign depends heavily on sound use of these technologies. They help to:

- communicate the candidate’s position effectively to voters;
- mobilize support among the electorate;
- optimize the use of resources;
- meet the campaign’s goals within the election cycle.

## Structure of the book

In this book we cover:

1. Core concepts and principles of election campaign technologies  
2. Methods of electorate analysis and strategy design  
3. How an election campaign is organized  
4. Communication strategies  
5. Working with different groups of voters  
6. Features of campaigns under different conditions  

## How to use the book

The book is written so that each chapter connects logically to the next. We recommend reading in order to get a full picture of the subject. At the same time, each chapter can be used on its own as a source on a specific aspect of campaign technologies.

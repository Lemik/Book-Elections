### Campaign Headquarters

The point of all staff activities is to relieve the candidate as much as possible in order to give him the opportunity to communicate with voters. We present here the most detailed model of the organizational structure, and the minimum model of the headquarters may consist of a candidate and one or two of his assistants. In this case, they distribute the functions described here among themselves.

Headquarters organization


To conduct election campaign professionally, it is necessary to create a headquarters.**Headquarters** is the main office, the main workplace for election campaign preparation. Here the candidate, the head of the election campaign, the “kitchen office” and the main staff can meet and develop various programs. It is also the command post from where all kinds of materials and information are sent out and flow into.

In order not to create a non-working environment, it is advisable to geographically separate the headquarters and the place of arrival of volunteers and voters. Functionally, two large blocks of issues and requirements for providing campaign headquarters are distinguished:

## 1) headquarters as an office:

headquarters location;

operating mode;

technical support (telephone communications, computer, transport, etc.) and life support for the headquarters;

material (leaflets, posters, etc.) support for the progress of the election campaign;

## 2) headquarters as an organizational structure:

- selection and training of management;
- creation of a management structure;
- control and stimulation of personnel.


What are the main functions that need to be implemented?

promoting the candidate's image;

communication with the media, preparation and placement of paid advertising materials; network campaign planning;

- management of the election campaign headquarters (material and technical support for work, selection and placement of personnel, setting tasks and monitoring implementation, stimulating (encouraging) employees); control over the candidate’s schedule, organization and conduct of planned specific events; operational management of election campaign resources; financial accounting;

legal support;

advisory support (a group of experts: sociologists, psychologists, journalists, directors, etc., who prepare information materials and give advice regarding the candidate’s image, holding various events, preparing the candidate for speeches, optimizing team actions, etc.);

ensuring the functioning of election campaign (communications, transport, food, stationery, consumables);

public relations;

special functions (security, intelligence, special events); production of propaganda materials (leaflets, booklets, posters: printing, posting).

Building a headquarters structure

The structure of the election campaign headquarters is built, as a rule, on a functional principle.

To do this, they describe the range of tasks facing the headquarters, and then determine the requirements for the team and specifically for each of its members heading a particular direction.

Here we consider the optimal organizational structure of the headquarters when conducting an election campaign of a candidate for deputy of the Verkhovna Rada in a single-mandate constituency (the number of voters is 150-180 thousand people).

This scheme can be significantly simplified during elections to local levels of government, and functions and responsibilities are simply redistributed among all election campaign organizers. When forming the structure of the headquarters, they usually proceed from the scale and tasks of the election campaign, as well as the material capabilities of the organizers.

Often, the candidate already has a circle of people (friends, acquaintances, relatives, subordinates) among whom the entire scope of work is distributed. It is important to really assess the skills and potential of everyone and arrange people in such a way as to use their knowledge and abilities with maximum effect.

The correct placement of people and timely coordination of their actions are the most important task of the head of the election campaign (see the diagram “Structure of the organization of the election headquarters”).

1. A specific team member (direction manager) is responsible for each direction (function). There is always only one person responsible for the implementation of plans, compliance with schedules, and carrying out events, while the implementation of this function can be carried out by a whole group.

A particular manager may sometimes be responsible for several areas, but never several managers should be responsible for the implementation of one function.2. Each team member reports to only one manager or the manager's manager.

3. The headquarters structure must provide for the ability to immediately replace a temporarily or permanently retired member



teams. Therefore, at least two people must have information on a specific area (most often, duplication of information is assigned to the head of the election campaign).4. The structure must provide for the possibility of distributing powers and responsibilities across all levels of management. The decision to purchase a ream of paper does not necessarily require the approval of the head of the investment committee.

5. The structure must have a level of complexity that maintains absolute and operational control of the election campaign. It is important to organize the work and interaction of election campaign members in such a way as not to lose control over the structure.

Recruitment

By what criteria is it advisable to recruit people for the team? There are many ways to select and train personnel: from among friends and acquaintances, by advertisement, attracting ready-made professional teams, training through specialized seminars, trainings, etc. Mandatory condition: people in leadership positions must enjoy the unconditional trust of the candidate. When recruiting personnel, five main groups of criteria are used to evaluate a potential applicant for the team.1. Individual and personal presence of the personal qualities, abilities and skills necessary for participation in election campaign, for example, efficiency, communication skills, prudence, organization, the ability to persuade, negotiate, the ability to manage people, moral and ethical qualities.2. Organizational and group: teamwork, ability to cooperate, synchronize.


3. Professional - experience in participating in/conducting ECs, knowledge of the electoral process, political marketing, management, general erudition.

4. Temporary resources: availability of free time and the opportunity to plunge headlong into the electoral process.5. Connections: the presence of acquaintances and connections in the media and communications, in enterprises and organizations, the degree of familiarity with the area.

Team formation

## Details of the training of election campaign workers depend on the candidate’s tactics and the type of his contacts with voters. However, there are key tactics for preparing a team:



Each employee is responsible for a specific area and receives his own task. Explain to him all the details of his work and show how it fits into the overall program;- convince employees to make every effort to complete their work. Get them to understand their place in the election campaign, the significance of their work, their role. Remember, you have very little time and will never have time to control all the actions of team members. Therefore, it is very important that everyone is imbued with a sense of responsibility and feels the excitement in achieving the common goal of victory;


give high praise to the actions of your employees, celebrate every result achieved, every successful event. Moral support and positive assessment of the actions of your employees are often more important than any material reward;

think over and organize a system of employee rewards in advance.

Once the team has been largely formed, a number of very specific resources and conditions are necessary for its successful work. Each unit (even if it is one person) must have a clear understanding of the goals of its activities, in particular: a specific activity plan;

- exercise control over your reserves and capabilities; minimal supply of necessary resources; own well-understood strategy and tactics; clearly identified competitors;

have a clear distinctive advantage;

a system of qualitative and quantitative performance assessments.

Head of election campaign

The head of the election campaign is a key person in the team who plans, organizes and implements all the activities necessary for the candidate’s victory. He takes responsibility for the entire organizational structure of the campaign and therefore has the broadest powers and the greatest power.

In election campaign this is the most important and responsible role. Success or defeat in elections depends on the knowledge, abilities and organization of this person. He must lead by uniting completely different people into an effective team.

In local government elections, the leader may be the only paid member of the team.



Ideally, he is the main and only source of “voice” for the candidate.

Requirements for a manager

There must be complete mutual understanding and unlimited trust between the head of the election campaign and the candidate. Achieving such unity, even in short election campaign, is extremely difficult. Therefore, it is necessary to approach the appointment to the post of head of the election campaign very carefully. Consider first of all the experience of the person you brought to work with you.

It is best to divide responsibilities between the candidate and the manager from the very beginning. The candidate must receive complete and up-to-date information about the progress of the election campaign from the manager and answers to all his questions. At the same time, one should not underestimate the authority and powers of the leader and interfere in matters that fall within his competence. The candidate must have absolute confidence in this person to allow him to approve decisions on critical issues.

In local elections, this role is most often performed by the candidate himself. If the candidate has no experience or is very busy, it makes sense to invite a specialist with good organizational skills and knowledge of the laws of the election game to this position.

Manager's tasks

## The main task of the head of the election campaign, in marketing terms, is to create an effective mechanism for “selling” a candidate to potential consumers and voters. Head of election campaign:

- organizes the preparation of the election campaign strategic plan, develops a network schedule of events, forms the election campaign budget, coordinates actions to create the image and program of the candidate;

has the decisive right to vote to authorize or veto certain events (the candidate himself in this case has the right of an advisory vote);

— administers and manages the election campaign, knows the main problems and opportunities of campaign participants. He hires and fires people, uses material and non-material incentives, solves problems of interaction between individuals and areas;




bears full responsibility for the budget and expenditure of funds;

constantly informs the candidate about the progress of the election campaign and the implementation of those programs that are outlined in the schedule; forms a team, places people and monitors the quality of their performance of their duties; encourages and punishes;

conducts negotiations on behalf of the candidate with public organizations and interest groups within the framework of the powers delegated to him;

organizes and coordinates meetings of team members, “kitchen cabinet”, monitors the provision of all election campaign members with the necessary information;

fully controls the daily work of the team during the election campaign, monitors and adjusts the schedule in accordance with the situation.

Confidants and support group

As a rule, popular and authoritative people from your district (city) are invited to become proxies. These people allow their names to be used as supporters of the candidate, although in reality they hardly work for the election campaign.

Most often, this role is offered to journalists, famous artists, scientists and teachers, heads of enterprises and organizations. By agreeing to become a confidant, they seem to be saying: “We know this person, we vouch for him!”

A candidate may have a limited number of trusted persons, so it is important to enlist the support of significant authorities, whose participation can have a positive impact on the image

candidate.

Your support group should be chaired by someone respected and well known in the area. His signature will then appear on information materials and appeals to voters. His fame and authority can ultimately significantly affect the degree of trust in leaflets and appeals.

"Kitchen cabinet>>>

This is the name of the group of election campaign managers who make up the “think tank” of the campaign; these are the candidate’s closest associates.“Kitchen Cabinet” is created for the prompt resolution of controversial issues, confidential discussions of organizational, financial



and other problems, a critical analysis of the course of the campaign, and possibly criticism of the statements and behavior of the candidate himself (of course, in his presence).

The “kitchen office” consists of the head of the election campaign, one or two leading ideologists of the campaign, an executive secretary, some heads of areas, a lawyer and a treasurer.

Analytical center

The functions of the analytical center include planning research for the district, analyzing and making recommendations, developing an election campaign strategy, and preparing candidates on fundamental issues.

The analytical center may also include freelance experts and consultants brought in from outside to resolve specific issues.

Think tank members also meet regularly (especially in the early stages of the election campaign) to assess the current situation, exchange information and adjust tactical plans. The head of the election campaign chairs these meetings and gives the final “go-ahead” for the holding of actions.

The composition of the analytical center is often determined spontaneously, but it is necessary that specialists from the most important areas participate in its meetings. It is also very useful to have consultants on hand in all possible areas to promptly resolve emerging issues. Ideally, it should be dominated by supporters of sociological (marketing) and psychological approaches.

The analytical center takes on creative functions: developing ideas, analyzing the electorate, developing the main positions of the strategy and some tactical aspects, as well as “debriefing”, analyzing successes and failures, developing action plans in crisis and critical situations.

### Treasurer

The treasurer is the chief accountant of the investment company. As a rule, an experienced accountant close to the candidate is invited to fill this position.

## He is responsible for accounts and financial documents related to the use of election campaign funds, maintains books of accounts, pays bills, prepares information on the use of funds and financial status reports. In addition, its functions include:


developing a strategy for obtaining funds for running an election campaign: compiling a list of potential cash donors, organizing a fundraising event;

together with the manager, drawing up estimates for the entire campaign and individual events;

opening a special bank account for the election fund in accordance with the established procedure and closing it at the end of the election campaign; maintaining full accounting records: registering all income, accounting for expenses, drawing up financial reports, issuing receipts for donations, etc.; financing of election campaign activities.

###Legal Advisor

Legal counsel must ensure that neither the candidate, nor the election campaign director, nor the treasurer are placed in a sensitive position. It is often necessary to solve problems of volunteers and ordinary voters, sign contracts, and prepare various documents.

The role of the legal adviser is especially important on election day: he instructs observers at polling stations and is on duty in the office so that at any time

moment to go to the polling station where violations have been observed in order to defend the interests of the candidate.

Legal counsel should determine whether any activities proposed by kitchen cabinet members comply with election laws.

Executive Secretary/Dispatcher

There must be a person at the headquarters who draws up the candidate’s work schedule and monitors its implementation. In Western election campaign this position is called scheduler (schedule daily routine), we have an analogue of this term - dispatcher, executive secretary, schedule manager. In some ECs, these functions are assumed by the election campaign head or press secretary.


The dispatcher is a person whom the candidate completely trusts and who is aware of all the affairs and problems of the candidate. This must be a very organized worker who knows how to pay attention to details and work with the public. Often someone from the candidate’s inner circle or relatives (wife, brother) is attracted to this post. Sometimes it makes sense to combine the positions of press secretary and dispatcher.

The dispatcher plans the candidate's time for the day, is aware of all his plans and prepares him accordingly for various activities.

The dispatcher provides the election campaign manager and individual employees with a weekly special schedule regarding the candidate’s actions. He must maintain a close working relationship with the candidate and with the people he trusts.

What does the dispatcher do?

Once the strategic election campaign schedule is developed, only the dispatcher makes adjustments and changes the candidate's current schedule. From this moment on, the candidate does not belong to himself, but obeys his dispatcher. The dispatcher is a filter. Both members of the headquarters and strangers, anyone who can take up the candidate’s time, must contact only the dispatcher, and there can be no exceptions to this rule. Only the dispatcher is fully responsible for the schedule and rhythm of the candidate’s life.

Here is a phrase that a candidate should remember and repeat when someone asks him for a meeting: “I do not know my exact schedule, please contact my dispatcher (secretary).”These simple words will save the candidate from a lot of trouble: he will never find himself in a position where he is forced to say “no”. This is the responsibility of the dispatcher. Let him get all the big shots.

Whenever a candidate declines an invitation, the manager or dispatcher must say so. In this case, the candidate will never act as a person who has not shown attention to any group or event.



The most important and appropriate reason to reject a proposal is that it does not comply with the current schedule. Sometimes the dispatcher may suggest a representative instead of a candidate.

Election Day Leader

Often ECs are lost due to the fact that some kind of failure occurs on election day. Therefore, a specially appointed person takes responsibility for preparing and conducting election day events (see “Election Day”).

## His responsibilities include:

- selection and training of observers who will monitor the voting process;

organization of a vote counting system;

planning the candidate's schedule for that day;

development of measures to stimulate the turnout of supporters at elections;

- coordination of the work of the headquarters on election day.

Security Director

## Its functions include:

selection, verification and control of personnel, as well as ensuring the loyalty of individual members;

tracking the actions of competitors, collecting information about tactics and teams of competitors, predicting their possible actions;

- development of measures aimed at ensuring the safety of members of the headquarters (during the candidate’s trip, campaigners’ campaigns);

suppression of possible information leakage, ensuring security of the headquarters;

- prevention of provocations from competitors. As a rule, this is a person familiar with the work experience of security structures or a former employee of internal affairs bodies. He is also responsible for the behavior of staff members in critical situations.

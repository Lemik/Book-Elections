### Candidate preparation

In this chapter you will find recommendations on how to competently prepare a candidate for victory. As a rule, all this advice does not make sense, because, firstly, there is simply no time for high-quality preparation of the candidate, secondly, it is almost impossible to change the usual behavior of the candidate and, thirdly, the people who promise you a “win-win campaign” and “universal techniques for winning the electorate” are in fact ordinary charlatans.

The candidate's relationship with the team

Experience shows that 40% of success depends on the coherence and professionalism of the team, 40% - on the individual abilities of the candidate and his discipline, as well as creative ideas related to the creation and promotion of the candidate’s image,


and 20% from external uncontrollable factors. Your job is to make the best use of your 80%.

However, in practice, often an overly anxious and demanding candidate can ruin the most professional game with his interference.

A lot depends on role and psychological preparation

candidate.

The candidate is called upon to realize his role as the main character of the election campaign, its inspirer and actual leader. He must provide trust and daily support to those around him. He is obliged to take care of his “image” in the eyes of voters and among his associates.



In addition, while trusting his team, the candidate must adhere to the following rules.

1. The candidate should have an idea of ​​the campaign as a whole, but not go into details. He forms the goals of his election campaign, participates in the development of the schedule and determines the strategy, but delegates all other responsibilities to his headquarters, and devotes himself to external contacts, establishing the necessary connections, creating coalitions and other matters that require personal participation.

2. The candidate strictly adheres to his schedule. Any exception becomes the rule.

3. The candidate should not be overworked, try not to get sick or become depressed. It is necessary to leave enough time between various activities - recreation, family, main work.

4. The candidate must be able to learn. If he feels that he is not connecting with the right people, he should discuss this with the “kitchen cabinet” and decide what needs to be changed in his behavior in order to achieve results. Not all people are born to be professional communicators, speakers, and leaders. But everything can be learned. If necessary, you should contact specialists - psychologists and sociologists.

5. A candidate must have unconditional trust in the people he recruits into his campaign. Special requirements are imposed on the head of the investment company, the manager and manager of funds. They seem to complement the candidate.

Roles and responsibilities within the team should be strictly assigned, and the candidate should be discouraged from interfering in the preparation and execution of events. The manager must be able to say “no” to a candidate if there is a violation of the established distribution of roles.

6. To successfully conduct an election campaign, the candidate needs the consent and support of all family members. The interests of the election campaign require that the candidate be away from the family for an extended period of time. Family members who understand the difficulties of conducting election campaign can ease tension, while arguments and misunderstandings will only increase tension.

7. The candidate must determine the degree of participation and role of close people in the election campaign. Without exaggerating the abilities of your loved ones, try to make the best use of their enthusiasm and devotion (especially appreciate the help of parents and children). The campaign staff should treat them with respect and strive to establish friendly relations.

Candidate requirements

The candidate must be a “great guy.”People need to feel that he cares about their problems, that he cares about his constituents. A candidate must be able to look at the world through the eyes of his voters, whose trust he is fighting for. Having understood their problems, the candidate offers his solution.

The candidate must clearly understand why he is seeking election to this position and what he can do if elected. The candidate must be a leader, organize and inspire people, and demonstrate the ability to be successful, persistent and goal-oriented.

It is important to be able to listen and sympathize. Your listeners and interlocutors should feel accepted and understood.

The candidate must be aware of all current issues and events that affect the situation in the district. Strive to choose exactly those problems that are in tune with your experience and your interests.

As practice shows, in elections to local councils the political orientation of the candidate does not matter. The choice happens

## based on:

internal feeling (emotional choice): “do I believe this person or not,” or

based on real facts (rational choice): “what he has already done, what he is doing now, what he promises to do.”

The candidate needs to have a whole list of “good deeds” of problems and issues that he encountered in the district and which he was able to successfully resolve. In addition, during the election campaign you can fulfill a number of requests that individual voters or groups of voters make to you.

However, don't start anything you can't finish, and make sure that fulfilling these requests won't take up all your time or interfere with the election campaign.


Candidate's image


Image is a set of statements and ideas that a person develops about a specific object or event through generalization and emotional experience. The image is of a subconscious, irrational nature, i.e. It is difficult for a person to determine why he treats a given object in this particular way. The image rewards the candidate with characteristics that are not always his real qualities, but are necessarily associated with him.

For example, in previous elections, the images of “the savior of the Fatherland,” “a man of action, a professional,” and “a fighter for justice” were actively used.

When creating an image, they rely on the already existing resources of a particular person, modifying them accordingly to facilitate acceptance by voters. Resources (components)

## image:

biographical facts;

family values ​​(family, children); professional abilities;

capacity, his potential as a politician;

character, temperament, habitual behavior patterns;

ability to transform, artistry, demonstrativeness.

Identification mechanism

Identification is a basic psychological reaction, which consists in the fact that we mentally equate ourselves with another person, as if “trying on” him for ourselves.

Unlike imitation, the identification mechanism is unconscious. A person unconsciously strives to become like another person, to accept his assessments and standards of behavior: most of the processes of learning and socialization (association in groups) of people are built on this.

There is a position that the majority of voters vote for a certain political leader not because they are fascinated by his program, but rather because they attribute to themselves certain traits of his personality, his views and standards of behavior. Explanations like: “I trust him because I like him” are clearly irrational.

In the process of identification, both personalities seem to merge into one. It is identification that underlies such supralogical feelings as intuitive trust, faith, and disposition. As shown



practice, people vote for a similar worldview and sympathy, for the trust they have in the candidate.

Main purpose of contacts

Based on this, we can formulate the main goal of the election campaign to create such an image and a mechanism for its promotion that will allow your candidate to become like and join as many voters as possible who will vote.

During the contact process, the candidate must demonstrate (and set) the framework and standard of the relationship: “I treat you this way, I trust you, I want to know your problems and help. So you can treat me the same way.”

Image formation

The election campaign strategy is always aimed at one single goal: victory on election day. Therefore, only a clearly developed, up-to-date image of your candidate can help achieve this goal.

Image allows you to identify the candidate as a person, distinguish him from the general crowd, and begin to feel a sense of trust in him. The image of a candidate is the impression that voters have about the candidate. The voter should feel that he knows the candidate, can talk to him, and trust him.

Types and levels of communication

There are two types of communication: logical

based

on a rational, persuasive approach, and emotional appealing primarily to the feelings and mood of the listeners. Politicians often overestimate the importance of logical communication. In the absence of other interaction elements, logical



## constructions are ethereal. Listeners (readers, viewers) do not have the motivation to act on the basis of bare logic. Therefore, when preparing a speech, it is necessary to take into account all levels of communication:

1) thematic - selection of a range of problems relevant to a specific audience and ways to solve them (“problem solution”);

2) stylistic


a set of means of persuasion, special oratorical techniques and forms of presenting material, the use of emotionally charged words in speech;

3) expressive use of voice modulations, changes in voice strength, pauses, marking important places, facial expressions and gestures;

4) linguistic - requirements for the construction of a phrase, for the structure of a sentence, the use of nominalizations, presubpositions, non-specific verbs.

Discussions “about politics” often seem like something very distant, incredibly boring and incomprehensible. Therefore, it is necessary to use “attention traps” by introducing emotionally or personally significant information into the text.

Logical constructs gain more meaning and meaning when the candidate is liked by the audience and the audience is interested in what he has to say. Therefore, in order to maintain interest, uninteresting political facts should be associated with something that concerns the voter personally. Candidates for the supreme legislative body should especially remember this, because lawmaking is very indirectly connected with solving the everyday everyday problems of the voter.

Communication structure

Communicating with each other, people simultaneously send their thoughts and desires in two completely different ways


verbal

and non-verbal. If non-verbal signals and speech impulses are incompatible, the flow of mutual understanding is disrupted.

Thus, regardless of the quality of the text of the speech, the overall effect of communication is achieved through a combination of factors such as facial expression, body movements during the speech, tone of voice, consistency of verbal and non-verbal sequence. Research shows that when speaking in front of a group of listeners, 55% of the impact is determined by body language (postures, gestures and eye contact), 38%


tone of voice



and only 7% - the content of what you are talking about. Tone of voice and body language determine whether the word “hello” will sound like a simple greeting, a threat, a suppression, or a playful exclamation.

If words are the content of a message, then posture, gestures, facial expressions and tone of voice represent the context in which the message is placed, and together they form the meaning of the communication. Therefore, the effect of information perception directly depends on the internal confidence and intentions of the person from whom the information comes, and is based mainly on non-speech factors.

How to prepare a speech

## Here is a list of what needs to be done to write a speech for a candidate:

- conduct a “preliminary interview” with the candidate, find out what questions he would like to pay attention to, what to touch upon in his speech. Select and discuss with him the main topic of the speech;

- record in as much detail as possible the words, turns of speech, style of presentation and gestures characteristic of the candidate. Be sure to weave these features into the text;

- find out about the location of the meeting and the composition of the audience; draw up a general plan, break the speech into three stages (introduction, main part, conclusion);

determine the goals and duration of each stage;

write for the human voice;

think about how to please the audience, not the candidate; remember the importance of repetition;- test your speech by ear.

### Introduction

Introduction - joining the audience and identifying with it. Talk to someone in the audience before your speech, find out more about the specifics of the audience, about their attitude to the problems that you are going to address. A good way to strengthen your connection with your audience is to mention someone present in your speech.

Main part

The effectiveness of a speech does not depend on its duration, but on the content and the relationship between what is said and how close and understandable it is to the listeners. A short, emotional speech “to the point” will more likely achieve its goal than a long two-hour one.

Basic speech mistakes

A politician often has the feeling that the facts speak for themselves. He presents the facts as an indifferent observer, expecting the listener to draw his own conclusions. The listener requires a more thorough study of the material with ready-made conclusions and solutions.

- Most of the time, candidates speak a language that is difficult to understand and full of terms. Using Summons

### Calls

These are appeals to emotional perception, which allow us to increase motivation and persuade us to take certain actions. Appeals should make it easier for the candidate to identify with the audience and help develop specific motivation (for example, “let's do X,” “together we can achieve X”).

To enhance motivation, the situation is depicted as critical, hopeless, on the verge of collapse, when only the immediate active participation of everyone together can correct the situation.

In this case, they turn not to the feeling of self-salvation (since the basic instinct of self-preservation will most likely turn on), but to the salvation of their children, relatives and friends, to the salvation of life values ​​(social protection, family, free medicine).

Often in such appeals the Motherland, the people and their history, universal human ideals and various lofty thoughts are mentioned. The task of calls is to elevate the actions of voters (not just “vote”, but “decide the fate of the country”, “choose your future”, “save the nation”).


However, appeals must be used very carefully and moderately; the speaker must ensure an increase in emotional tension. If a candidate fails to “ignite” listeners with the help of appeals, he loses more in the eyes of voters than if he convinced

using logic.

What is NLP

### Psychotechnology

In the process of creating political advertising, modern communication psychotechnologies occupy an important place. This is a set of methods, techniques and methods of conducting directed influence in order to solve certain problems. The main goal of any contact is to cause the desired change in the behavior of the interlocutor through your actions. This is achieved by transferring information.

NLP is neuro-linguistic programming, a direction in psychology that studies the patterns of human behavior, develops optimal interaction techniques and builds models of effective activity.


The main questions that NLP seeks to answer are: “How does it work? Could this work better? How can this be taught? There are a lot of people who can do something very well. NLP's job is to explore how they do it WELL.Having only one way to do something (be it cross stitch or speaking in front of an audience) is a lack of choice. Sometimes it will work out, and sometimes it won't. After all, situations can always arise that your usual way of behavior is not able to cope with and will even limit your freedom of action.


It is clear that if you constantly do what you have always done, then you will get what you have always received. But man strives for improvement and perfection. He is looking for new forms of behavior, new skills, new reserves. And the wider his choice, the greater the chances of success.

Mastery of NLP psychotechnologies allows you to get more choices and radically expand your repertoire of behavior.

Features of performing on television

Television is one of the most influential (and at the same time most expensive) means of contact with voters.

Appearing on television allows you to create a powerful presence and introduce voters to the candidate better. Television is used especially effectively at the name identification stage. It’s good when the audience’s attention is pre-warmed (preliminary information about “good deeds”, statements, rumors) and a desire is formed to “get to know each other better.”

The viewer can, in a calm environment, examine the candidate’s facial expression, determine how sincere he is, and pay attention to his non-verbal behavior.

When planning a television speech, you need to clearly understand how exactly the viewer will listen to your candidate (sitting in a comfortable chair, in a familiar environment near the TV) and what exactly you want to say. It is necessary to study in detail the candidate’s behavior in front of the camera.





When preparing for a speech, use the technique of a “failure interview” when, in an environment as close as possible to a studio, provocative questions, barbed remarks and even insults are thrown at him from all sides, to which the candidate must respond instantly and wittily. This will help teach the candidate to behave at ease and naturally in front of the camera.

Speaking in the studio, the candidate is deprived of feedback from the audience and cannot regulate the emotional tension of his speech. Therefore, seat a small interested audience in the studio to which the candidate will address. By observing their reaction, the speaker assesses the expected reaction of television viewers.
